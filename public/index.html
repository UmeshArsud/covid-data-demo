    Know the basics

    🤒 Common symptoms

    Fever, dry cough, tiredness, sore throat and loss of taste or smell. Some people have no symptoms.

    🧼 Protect yourself

    Stay vaccinated, wash hands for 20 seconds, wear a mask in crowded indoor places and ventilate rooms.

    🏠 If you feel unwell

    Rest, drink fluids and test if you can. Call a doctor if you have trouble breathing, chest pain or confusion.

    📰 Reliable sources

    Check the WHO website and your health ministry before sharing news.
